# BE-38 A rule coverage (2026-09-13)

The dated selector lives in `src/engine/rules`. Amounts are nominal and pinned in source control. It does not consult the network, system date or current remote tables. A published pack wins; an unpublished future year requires an explicit annual-rate argument, records `assumedFutureRule`, and applies rounding once to the pinned base for the requested year. Missing past years fail closed. The projection engine has **not** yet switched to per-year packs; it still uses its existing 2026 constants in every projected year. The disclosure in both input modes states this limitation.

The 2026 snapshot deliberately preserves existing values, including PE/MB discrepancies, rather than silently changing results in this foundation slice. The generic [CRA 2026 rate table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/tax-rates-brackets/current-year.html) lists PE's fourth threshold as $142,520 and MB's first two thresholds as $47,564/$101,200; the legacy engine holds $142,250 and $47,000/$100,000. The dedicated, fixed-year [CRA T4032-MB 2026 guide](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/cra-arc/migration/cra-arc/tx/bsnss/tpcs/pyrll/t4032/2026/t4032-mb-1-26e.pdf) itself lists $47,000/$100,000 and $15,780 BPA. This is a source conflict, not grounds for an automatic change. The MB pack stores both URLs and the conflict text. BE-38 B owns legal reconciliation and a new rule version before calculation changes.

Each pack maps its bracket, BPA, benefit-amount and AFNI-threshold fields to their own primary URLs. The 2025 federal full-year 14.5% rate is in the dated [Finance Canada 2025 tax-cut release](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2025/05/delivering-a-middle-class-tax-cut.html); the [CRA T4032-ON July 2025 PDF](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/cra-arc/migration/cra-arc/tx/bsnss/tpcs/pyrll/t4032/2025/t4032-on-7-25e.pdf) documents the 2025 federal/ON thresholds and BPAs. For 2026, each jurisdiction's dated T4032 PDF documents federal figures and non-QC provincial brackets/BPA; the [Quebec 2026 fiscal-parameters PDF](https://www.finances.gouv.qc.ca/Budget_et_mise_a_jour/maj/documents/AUTFR_RegimeImpot2026.pdf) documents QC brackets/BPA. The [CRA 2025–26 CCB calculation sheet](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/canada-child-benefit/canada-child-benefit-ccb-calculation-sheet-july-2025-june-2026-payments-2024-tax-year.html) documents both CCB amounts and thresholds. For 2026–27, the [dated federal release](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/news/2026/07/canada-child-benefit-payments-increasing-in-2026-2027.html) documents amounts; CRA's [indexation table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/frequently-asked-questions-individuals/adjustment-personal-income-tax-benefit-amounts.html) has a specifically labeled 2026 column with the $38,237/$82,847 thresholds. That last page has a rolling URL; the pinned values and independent fixture preserve the 2026 evidence if its displayed columns later change. A fixed-year CRA calculation sheet for 2026–27 was not available at verification.
