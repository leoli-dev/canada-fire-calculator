# BE-38 A rule coverage (2026-09-13)

The dated selector lives in `src/engine/rules`. Amounts are nominal and pinned in source control. It does not consult the network, system date or current remote tables. A published pack wins; an unpublished future year requires an explicit annual-rate argument, records `assumedFutureRule`, and applies rounding once to the pinned base for the requested year. Missing past years fail closed. Future years also fail closed when an indexed tax bracket collides with a frozen successor (for example ON in 2042 at 2.1%) or benefit values overflow to nonfinite amounts; neither selector returns a pack that fails publication validation. The projection engine has **not** yet switched to per-year packs; it still uses its existing 2026 constants in every projected year. The disclosure in both input modes states this limitation.

The 2026 snapshot no longer preserves the PE/MB discrepancies it was seeded with; BE-38 B3 resolved both and the resolution is recorded in `sourceResolutions` and in each pack's `sourceConflict`. The generic [CRA 2026 rate table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/tax-rates-brackets/current-year.html) lists MB's first two thresholds as $47,564/$101,200; the dedicated, fixed-year [CRA T4032-MB 2026 guide](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/cra-arc/migration/cra-arc/tx/bsnss/tpcs/pyrll/t4032/2026/t4032-mb-1-26e.pdf) lists $47,000/$100,000 and a $15,780 BPA, and its own text explains that Manitoba paused indexation for 2026. The dedicated guide governs: MB retained $47,000/$100,000 and $15,780, and **no number changed**. For PE, the [January 2026 T4032-PE PDF, Chart 2](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/cra-arc/migration/cra-arc/tx/bsnss/tpcs/pyrll/t4032/2026/t4032-pe-1-26e.pdf) carried the superseded $142,250 fourth threshold; the [July 2026 T4032-PE](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/cra-arc/migration/cra-arc/tx/bsnss/tpcs/pyrll/t4032/2026/t4032-pe-7-26e.pdf) and CRA's generic rates page both give $142,520, and the July guide states that PE's new over-$200,000 bracket is 20% for 2026 and subsequent years (the 21% it also prints is a six-month prorated withholding rate). The pack now prices $142,520 with 17.62%/19%/20%, which lowers PE provincial tax by $3.726 at $142,520 and above, phasing in from nil at $142,250. Both sources remain attached to the provincial-brackets field. BE-38 B3 owns the per-jurisdiction coverage matrix that discloses which credits each priced number includes; the PE threshold move above is the only calculation change it made.

Each pack maps its bracket, BPA, benefit-amount and AFNI-threshold fields to their own primary URLs. The 2025 federal full-year 14.5% rate is in the dated [Finance Canada 2025 tax-cut release](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2025/05/delivering-a-middle-class-tax-cut.html); the [CRA T4032-ON July 2025 PDF](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/cra-arc/migration/cra-arc/tx/bsnss/tpcs/pyrll/t4032/2025/t4032-on-7-25e.pdf) supplies the upper federal rates, federal/ON thresholds and BPAs. Both sources are attached specifically to federal-bracket provenance. For 2026, each jurisdiction's dated January T4032 PDF documents federal figures; the [CRA July BC guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/payroll/t4032-payroll-deductions-tables/t4032bc-july/t4032bc-july-general-information.html) explains the 5.60% full-year BC first rate, while its July payroll withholding table prorates to 6.14%; the [CRA July NL guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/payroll/t4008-payroll-deductions-supplementary-tables/t4008nl-july/t4008nl-july-general-information.html) explains the $13,094 BPA effective January 1. Other provincial sources remain the dated T4032 PDFs, except the [Quebec 2026 fiscal-parameters PDF](https://www.finances.gouv.qc.ca/Budget_et_mise_a_jour/maj/documents/AUTFR_RegimeImpot2026.pdf). The [CRA 2025–26 CCB calculation sheet](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/canada-child-benefit/canada-child-benefit-ccb-calculation-sheet-july-2025-june-2026-payments-2024-tax-year.html) documents both CCB amounts and thresholds. For 2026–27, the [dated federal release](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/news/2026/07/canada-child-benefit-payments-increasing-in-2026-2027.html) documents $8,157/$6,883 amounts; CRA's [indexation table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/frequently-asked-questions-individuals/adjustment-personal-income-tax-benefit-amounts.html) has a specifically labeled 2026 column with the $38,237/$82,847 thresholds. That last page has a rolling URL; the independent fixture pins all four values if its displayed columns later change. A fixed-year CRA calculation sheet for 2026–27 was not available at verification.
